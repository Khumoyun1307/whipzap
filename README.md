# WhipZap

Whip your AI coding agent into shape.

WhipZap is a small tray app for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Crack the whip and it plays a whip-crack sound, flashes a strike across your screen, and types a short taunt such as `FASTER!` into the terminal or editor you locked as the target. Turn on auto mode and it cracks on its own at random intervals with escalating insults while your agent grinds through a long task.

It never interrupts the agent. Every whip is delivered as a normal queued message or as a `/btw` side question, so your agent keeps working and your work is never lost.

**Status: pre-alpha, under active development.** Not yet published to npm. See `docs/` for the full specification and `docs/ROADMAP.md` for progress.
